How we told them apart

A report's cause depends on its tenant: the locale it writes dates in, and the booking form it runs. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter's browser. The table shows how far it is off: intake tagged some parser reports hint, and some hint reports parser.

The one-day reports come from the desk's own notes (oneDayMove in desk-notes.json). Read them: "Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May", "Every booking in June is one day off", "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday". None of them swaps a month and a day. We think they point at time zones. The export cannot prove it, so #3 asks someone to check.
